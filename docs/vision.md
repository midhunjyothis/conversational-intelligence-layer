# Vision

Build a semantic-logic intelligence layer that converts raw human conversations into
structured meaning, shared context, and executable clarity. This layer becomes the
foundation for a future organizational OS where communication, planning, and decision-
making are precise, aligned, and low-effort.

Long-term: A context-aware assistant that understands teams, mediates intent, resolves
misalignment, and turns dialog into narrative, plans, actions, and dashboards.
