# Research Foundations

The engine is grounded in:
- Uncertainty Reduction Theory → clarity, specificity, reduced ambiguity
- Expectancy Violations Theory → tone, respect, register awareness
- Social Penetration Theory → calibrated empathy and acknowledgment
- Interactive/Transactional Models → alignment loops and shared context
- Media Richness Theory → when to escalate format (text → call)

Goal: convert human conversation into stable semantic and logical structures that can
drive action, planning, dashboards, and organizational clarity.
